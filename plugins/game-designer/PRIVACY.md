# Privacy

AI Game Designer (the `game-designer` plugin and its skills) does not collect, store, or send any
personal data.

- The skills are Markdown instructions that your AI agent reads.
- The bundled Python scripts run on your own machine, read only the files you give them, and
  write results to the output folder you choose. They make no network requests.
- There is no telemetry, no analytics, no account, and no server operated by this project.
- Whatever your AI agent does with your conversation is governed by that agent's own privacy
  policy (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google), not by this plugin.

The playable demos in `games/` are static web pages. When you open them in a browser, they
load fonts from Google Fonts (`fonts.googleapis.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com`), and they may save
settings and progress in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to this project.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/bunhine0452/ai-game-designer/issues
